# Meowmarism Contributors

This file is the official project-level contributor and governance record referenced by the Meowmarism License and the Meowmarism Contributor & Governance Agreement.

Contributor attribution must not be removed or rewritten merely because somebody stops participating in the project.

Each contributor may choose the reasonable public name, pseudonym, or username used for their own attribution.

## Attribution Rules

- Existing Recognized Contributor entries must be preserved in redistributions and forks.
- A contributor may request a different reasonable attribution name for themselves.
- Nobody else may arbitrarily remove, rename, replace, or hide another contributor's attribution while that contributor's work remains materially represented.
- Commit-level authorship remains part of project history even when a contribution is too trivial to create Governance Contributor status.
- A trivial or bad-faith change made primarily to obtain governance rights does not create those rights.
