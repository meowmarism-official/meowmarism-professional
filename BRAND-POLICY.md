# Meowmarism Brand Policy 1.0

Copyright (c) 2026 HexedMaya. All rights reserved.

This Policy describes the limited circumstances in which third parties may use the official Meowmarism name, logo, and brand assets.

Nothing in this Policy grants ownership of the Meowmarism name, logo, artwork, or other brand assets.

## 1. Permitted Attribution Use

The official Meowmarism logo and name may be used solely to truthfully identify that a product, service, server, hosting platform, website, project, or other offering:

* uses Meowmarism;
* includes Meowmarism;
* is based on Meowmarism;
* is powered by Meowmarism;
* is compatible with Meowmarism.

Permitted wording includes:

* "Powered by Meowmarism"
* "Uses Meowmarism"
* "Based on Meowmarism"
* "Built with Meowmarism"
* "Compatible with Meowmarism"

The official logo may appear next to such wording.

It may also be used in:

* website footers;
* credits pages;
* integrations pages;
* documentation;
* compatibility lists;
* technology or software-stack sections.

The attribution may link to the official Meowmarism website or official repository.

## 2. Conditions for Logo Use

When using the Meowmarism logo:

* only an official logo variant provided by the Meowmarism Project may be used;
* the logo must not be distorted, redrawn, recreated, or materially altered;
* official light, dark, monochrome, or other provided variants may be used as intended;
* reasonable resizing is permitted;
* sufficient spacing must be maintained so the logo is not visually merged with another brand;
* the use must accurately describe the relationship with Meowmarism.

## 3. Prohibited Use

The Meowmarism name or logo may not be used:

* as a third party's own company logo;
* as the primary branding of an unrelated product or service;
* as a favicon or application icon representing a third party;
* as a social-media avatar representing a third party;
* in a way that implies ownership of Meowmarism;
* in a way that falsely implies partnership, sponsorship, certification, approval, affiliation, or endorsement;
* in connection with unrelated products or services;
* on merchandise for sale without separate written permission;
* in a misleading or deceptive manner;
* in a way that suggests an unofficial version is the official Meowmarism Project.

## 4. Modified and Unofficial Versions

Use of the Meowmarism name or logo does not override any requirement of the Meowmarism License.

If a third party distributes, hosts, or publicly offers a Modified Version of Meowmarism, the unofficial-version, naming, attribution, rebranding, and non-affiliation requirements of the applicable Meowmarism License continue to apply.

A third party may not use the official logo to make an unofficial Modified Version appear official.

Where required by the Meowmarism License, wording such as:

"Unofficial Meowmarism"

or:

"Based on Meowmarism. Unofficial independent modified version."

must remain clearly visible.

## 5. Rebranded Projects

A rebranded project based on Meowmarism may use the official Meowmarism logo only as attribution.

For example:

> CoolPanel
> Based on [Meowmarism logo] Meowmarism

is permitted when all requirements of the Meowmarism License and this Brand Policy are satisfied.

The Meowmarism logo must not become the primary identity of the rebranded project.

## 6. No Endorsement

Permitted attribution use does not mean that the Meowmarism Project:

* endorses the third party;
* approves its product or service;
* provides support for it;
* sponsors it;
* certifies it;
* operates it;
* is affiliated with it.

A third party must not state or imply otherwise without separate written permission.

## 7. Third-Party Branding

This Policy applies only to branding controlled by the Meowmarism Project.

Third-party logos, trademarks, names, icons, or other branding appearing in Meowmarism projects remain subject to the terms and rights of their respective owners.

This Policy does not grant any rights in third-party branding.

## 8. Misleading or Non-Compliant Use

The Meowmarism Project may require correction or removal of use that:

* violates this Policy;
* violates the Meowmarism License;
* is misleading;
* falsely implies official status or endorsement;
* materially alters or misuses the official logo.

Limited attribution permission under this Policy ends for uses that do not comply with these conditions.
