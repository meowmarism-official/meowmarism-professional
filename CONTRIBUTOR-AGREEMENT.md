# Meowmarism Contributor & Governance Agreement 1.0

Version 1.0

This Agreement applies to contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion in the official Meowmarism Project.

Its purpose is to keep accepted contributions publicly maintainable, preserve contributor credit, prevent the unilateral sale, proprietary relicensing, or closing of community work, and ensure that trivial contributions cannot be used to manufacture project-control rights.

"Licensor" has the meaning given in the Meowmarism License (HexedMaya).

## 1. Contribution

A "Contribution" is original material intentionally submitted for inclusion in official Meowmarism, including source code, patches, pull requests, commits, tests, documentation, translations, assets, designs, configuration, scripts, or other project material.

By submitting a Contribution, you represent that you have the legal right to submit it and grant the rights described here.

Do not knowingly submit material you do not have permission to contribute.

## 2. Contributor Authorship

A Contributor remains the author of their own original Contribution to the extent provided by applicable law.

This Agreement does not erase, replace, or falsely transfer legal authorship.

Authorship of a Contribution does not make the Contributor the owner of Meowmarism as a whole and does not by itself grant project-control rights.

## 3. Attribution Name

Each accepted Contributor may choose the reasonable public name used for their attribution.

This may be:

- a real name;
- a pseudonym;
- a GitHub username;
- another reasonable identifier chosen by that Contributor.

A Contributor is not required to publish their legal name merely to receive credit.

A Contributor may later request that future official attribution use a different reasonable identifier.

No other person may arbitrarily rename, delete, replace, or hide that Contributor's attribution while their work remains materially represented in the project.

## 4. Public Nature of Accepted Contributions

Meowmarism is a source-available project.

By submitting a Contribution that is accepted into official Meowmarism, the Contributor understands and agrees that the accepted Contribution may become publicly accessible and may remain publicly accessible.

Accepted Contributions may appear in:

- public source repositories;
- public commit history;
- source archives;
- official releases;
- mirrors;
- forks made under the Meowmarism License;
- project documentation.

A Contributor must not submit material that must remain confidential.

Passwords, secrets, private keys, credentials, confidential company information, or personal data not intended for publication must not be submitted.

Leaving the project does not by itself require removal of previously accepted Contributions from public source history or releases.

## 5. Rights Granted for Project Operation

For each accepted Contribution, the Contributor grants the official Meowmarism Project and the Licensor a worldwide, royalty-free, continuing right, to the maximum extent permitted by law, to:

- reproduce and store the Contribution;
- publish and make it publicly available;
- compile and execute it;
- maintain, repair, refactor, modify, and adapt it;
- translate it;
- combine it with other project material;
- remove or replace portions during normal development;
- distribute it as part of Meowmarism;
- include it in future Meowmarism releases;
- distribute it under the Meowmarism License;
- allow downstream users the rights granted by the Meowmarism License, including its permission for commercial use;
- transfer these project-operation rights to a lawful successor only as permitted by this Agreement.

Contributors permit commercial use of their Contributions consistent with the Meowmarism License, but do not grant authority to proprietary-license, close-source, or separately sell their Contributions as a software product outside the permissions of that License.

These rights are granted so the project can continue to exist and be maintained even if an individual Contributor stops participating.

To the extent legally possible, rights already validly granted for an accepted Contribution are not revoked merely because the Contributor later leaves the project, deletes an account, changes a username, or disagrees with a maintainer.

Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

## 6. Rights Not Granted

A Contributor does not grant the Licensor an unrestricted right to:

- sell the Contributor's Contribution as a software product;
- license the Contributor's Contribution under proprietary terms, or under terms that charge for the Software itself (Software Monetization under the Meowmarism License);
- convert the Contributor's Contribution into proprietary software;
- place the Contributor's Contribution, as part of the Software itself, behind paid access (Software Monetization under the Meowmarism License);
- grant exclusive rights over the Contribution that would restrict the rights of others under the Meowmarism License;
- use the Contribution to sell or separately monetize the Software itself, including as a paid software product or as a paid service whose primary product is access to Meowmarism itself (Software Monetization under the Meowmarism License);
- remove mandatory attribution;
- pretend somebody else authored the Contribution.

This does not prevent the official project or anyone else from operating a commercial service, hosting, support, consulting, or products that merely use Meowmarism as a tool or component, as permitted by the Meowmarism License.

No maintainer, Licensor, repository owner, organization owner, domain owner, server owner, or successor receives those rights merely by controlling project infrastructure.

## 7. Permanent Restrictions on Community Work

The rights granted under this Agreement do not include authority to sell the Software or community Contributions as a software product, to proprietary-license them, or to close their source.

The following cannot be authorized merely by a governance vote:

- sale of community Contributors' copyright or authorship;
- sale or exclusive licensing of rights in Contributions that were never granted;
- proprietary relicensing of community Contributions;
- removal of contributor authorship history;
- conversion of community Contributions into closed-source or proprietary software.

If a future action legally requires rights that a Contributor did not grant under this Agreement, those rights must be obtained separately from every affected rights holder.

The Licensor or a governance vote cannot create rights the project does not possess.

## 8. Contributor Record

Official Meowmarism shall maintain a public CONTRIBUTORS.md file or equivalent contributor record.

Every Recognized Contributor whose accepted work remains materially represented in Meowmarism must remain represented in that record.

A Contributor may change the reasonable public identifier used for their own attribution.

A maintainer may not remove somebody merely because they stopped participating, lost repository access, disagreed with another maintainer, changed accounts, or are no longer personally associated with the project.

## 9. Contributor Classes

Meowmarism distinguishes between the following roles.

### Accepted Contributor

A person whose original Contribution has been accepted into official Meowmarism.

Accepted Contributors retain accurate authorship history.

### Recognized Contributor

An Accepted Contributor whose Contribution is meaningful enough to warrant permanent project-level attribution in CONTRIBUTORS.md.

### Governance Contributor

A Recognized Contributor with substantial, meaningful involvement who has been explicitly granted governance status in CONTRIBUTORS.md.

Governance status is based on actual project involvement, not raw line count.

## 10. Trivial Contributions Do Not Create Governance Rights

A person does not become a Governance Contributor merely by:

- correcting one typo;
- changing one line;
- changing whitespace or formatting;
- changing a version number;
- making a trivial configuration adjustment;
- submitting an automated dependency update;
- renaming something without substantial work;
- deliberately splitting a trivial change into multiple commits or pull requests;
- making a change primarily to obtain voting or veto rights.

The actual substance, originality, significance, and sustained involvement are considered.

A trivial Contribution may still have accurate commit-level authorship, but it does not automatically create permanent governance authority.

## 11. Granting Governance Status

Governance Contributor status must be explicitly recorded in CONTRIBUTORS.md or an equivalent governance record.

Relevant factors may include:

- substantial technical Contributions;
- repeated meaningful Contributions;
- responsibility for an important project component;
- sustained maintenance;
- significant documentation, design, testing, or security work;
- other meaningful long-term involvement.

No single numeric line-count threshold controls the decision.

A person cannot grant themselves Governance Contributor status merely by submitting code.

## 12. Protected Project Decisions

The following are "Protected Project Decisions":

- changing the primary project license;
- materially weakening the restriction on Software Monetization, or materially changing the balance between permitted commercial use and restricted Software Monetization in the Meowmarism License;
- materially weakening the public-source requirement;
- materially weakening mandatory contributor attribution;
- materially weakening the unofficial-fork and non-affiliation rules;
- transferring maintenance of the official project to another person or entity;
- granting official status to an independently maintained fork;
- changing this governance model in a way that reduces existing Governance Contributor protections;
- attempting a major change in project purpose that materially changes the basis on which Contributors contributed.

A Protected Project Decision requires the explicit approval of all current Governance Contributors.

Silence does not count as approval.

Approval must be recorded in a durable form associated with the project.

Even unanimous governance approval cannot grant rights that individual authors never granted to the project.

## 13. No Sale by a Single Owner

Possession or control of any of the following does not create ownership of Contributors' work:

- the GitHub repository or organization;
- administrator permissions;
- the domain;
- servers or infrastructure;
- release signing keys;
- package accounts;
- social accounts;
- project branding;
- the role of maintainer of the official project.

A person therefore cannot take over project infrastructure and lawfully claim ownership of all Meowmarism code or the right to sell everybody's work.

Each Contributor's rights and the limits of the rights they granted continue to apply.

## 14. Project Succession

Normal project succession is allowed only as a Protected Project Decision.

A successor maintainer must expressly accept:

- the Meowmarism License;
- this Contributor & Governance Agreement;
- all existing contributor attribution;
- existing governance protections;
- the rules of the Meowmarism License on commercial use and Software Monetization;
- the limits of the rights originally granted by Contributors.

The successor receives no broader rights than the project lawfully possessed before the succession.

A succession may not be used as a disguised commercial sale or proprietary conversion.

## 15. No Contributor Erasure During Succession

A successor may not restart contributor history or remove existing Recognized Contributors.

Project succession changes maintainership. It does not rewrite authorship.

A successor may not replace the contributor record with only their own name or present the project as if they created work authored by earlier Contributors.

## 16. Contributor Withdrawal

A Contributor may stop participating at any time.

Leaving does not automatically revoke project-operation rights already validly granted for accepted Contributions.

The project may continue to maintain, modify, publish, and distribute those Contributions under the Meowmarism License.

Attribution obligations continue to apply.

Mandatory legal rights remain unaffected.

## 17. Rejected Contributions

Submitting a Contribution does not require the project to accept, merge, publish, or maintain it.

If a Contribution is rejected and never incorporated into official Meowmarism, this Agreement does not give the project a general right to use that rejected material outside what is necessary to review the submission.

Public development-platform records may remain visible according to the platform's operation and policies.

## 18. No Automatic Right to Payment or Ownership

Contribution alone does not create:

- employment;
- a contractor relationship;
- partnership;
- ownership of the whole project;
- entitlement to project donations;
- entitlement to profits;
- entitlement to payment.

Separate written arrangements may be made where appropriate.

## 19. Acceptance

A Contribution may be accepted into official Meowmarism only if the Contributor has accepted the applicable version of this Agreement in a manner designated by the project.

For pull requests or equivalent submissions, the project may require an explicit statement such as:

"By submitting this contribution for inclusion in official Meowmarism, I agree to the Meowmarism Contributor & Governance Agreement 1.0."

Finding, forking, or privately modifying Meowmarism does not make a person a Contributor to the official project.

## 20. Amendments

Ordinary administrative text may be clarified without removing previously granted Contributor protections.

Any amendment that materially changes:

- the rules on commercial use and Software Monetization;
- contributor attribution;
- governance rights;
- project succession;
- the scope of rights Contributors granted;

is itself a Protected Project Decision.

Existing Contributors remain governed by the version they accepted unless they validly accept a replacement or applicable law provides otherwise.

No new version of this Agreement can retroactively create rights that a Contributor never granted.

## 21. Mandatory Law

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to waive, transfer, or exclude rights that applicable law makes non-waivable or non-transferable.

If a provision cannot operate exactly as written, it shall operate to the greatest legally permitted extent consistent with its intended purpose.

## 22. Governing Law

To the extent permitted by applicable law, this Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.

## 23. Contributor Model

Author: the person who actually created original work.

Accepted Contributor: an author whose Contribution was accepted into official Meowmarism.

Recognized Contributor: an Accepted Contributor with meaningful work that receives permanent project-level attribution.

Governance Contributor: a Recognized Contributor with substantial project involvement and explicit governance status.

This structure preserves credit for real work while preventing somebody from manufacturing permanent project control through an intentionally trivial change.
